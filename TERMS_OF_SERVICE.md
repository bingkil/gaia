GAIA is a situational-awareness tool. It aggregates public earthquake, volcanic,
ash-advisory, and satellite thermal-anomaly feeds and presents them on a map.

- GAIA is **not** an official early warning system for any hazard.

  decisions.** Always follow instructions from your local civil protection, geological
  survey, or emergency management authority.
